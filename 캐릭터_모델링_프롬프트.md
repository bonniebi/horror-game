# 여덟 번째 실종자 — 3D 캐릭터 모델링 프롬프트

3D 생성 AI(Meshy, Tripo, Rodin 등)에 붙여 넣을 수 있는 영어 프롬프트예요.
각 캐릭터마다 **Prompt / Negative prompt / 메모**로 나눴어요.

## 공통 팁
- **리깅(뼈대)할 캐릭터는 A-pose 또는 T-pose**로 뽑으세요. 나중에 걷기·쫓기 애니메이션을 붙이기 쉬워요.
- 모든 프롬프트에 아래 공통 꼬리말을 붙이면 결과가 안정적이에요:
  `single character, full body, centered, neutral grey background, PBR textures, game-ready mesh, realistic horror style`
- 공통 Negative prompt:
  `cartoon, chibi, cute, anime, low detail, multiple characters, cropped, text, watermark, extra heads`
- 게임 속 키를 적어 두었어요 (보통 사람 1.6m 기준). 엔진에 넣을 때 이 높이에 맞추면 돼요.

---

## 1. 머리카락의 여자 (병동 · 마지막 방)
키 약 2.6m · 빛을 보고 발소리를 듣는 추격자

**Prompt**
```
A horrifying emaciated pale woman, 2.6 meters tall, unnaturally long thin limbs, skin stretched over visible ribs, collarbones and spine, sunken belly, wearing a torn, blood-stained and filthy hospital gown with holes and a ragged hem, long wet black hair hanging over most of her face, deep dark sunken eye sockets with tiny pinprick white glints, thin streaks of blood tears running down her cheeks, narrow jaw, slightly open mouth with small teeth, extremely long jointed fingers with dark cracked nails, bare bony feet with long toes, faint purple veins and bruises under translucent skin, standing in A-pose
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cartoon, cute, anime, clean skin, healthy body, short hair, modern clothes, shoes, smiling
```
**메모**: 머리카락이 얼굴 앞으로 몇 가닥 흘러내려야 무서워요. 피눈물·멍은 텍스처로 처리하면 돼요.

---

## 2. 눈 없는 것 (병동 · 기계실)
키 약 2.7m · 보지 못하고 모든 소리를 듣는다

**Prompt**
```
A blind, eyeless humanoid creature, 2.7 meters tall, hunched forward, grey-brown leathery skin, elongated ridged skull stretched upward and backward, smooth skin completely covering where the eyes should be with shallow sockets, a vertical split mouth running from nose to chin lined with rows of small sharp yellowed teeth, extremely prominent ribs and knobbly spine, sunken stomach, very long arms reaching below the knees, long bony fingers, thin legs with sharp knees, no clothing, standing in A-pose
```
**Negative prompt**
```
eyes, eyeballs, hair, clothes, cute, cartoon, muscular, smooth body
```
**메모**: 혀를 차는 "딸깍" 소리로 위치를 드러내는 괴물이라, 입 주변을 크게 강조하면 좋아요.

---

## 3. 하얀 인형 (놀이방)
키 약 2.5m · 보고 있으면 멈추고, 안 보면 다가온다

**Prompt**
```
A tall eerie porcelain mannequin doll, 2.5 meters tall, slender featureless body made of off-white glazed ceramic, visible ball joints at shoulders, elbows, hips and knees, a thin seam line around the waist, the surface covered in fine black cracks and chipped-off pieces revealing dark hollow interior, smooth egg-shaped head with two empty black hollow eye holes, small nose bump, no mouth, long thin fingers, dirty brown stains on the porcelain, standing perfectly still in A-pose
```
**Negative prompt**
```
clothes, hair, painted face, smile, cute doll, cartoon, colorful, shiny new
```
**메모**: 자세를 여러 개(팔 든 자세, 고개 꺾인 자세) 따로 뽑아두면 "볼 때마다 자세가 바뀌는" 연출에 그대로 쓸 수 있어요.

---

## 4. 검은 그림자 (놀이방 · 마지막 방)
키 약 2.6m · 어둠 속에서 벽을 뚫고 다가오는 귀신

**Prompt**
```
A tall floating ghost made of black smoke and tattered shadow cloth, 2.6 meters tall, no legs, the lower body dissolving into ragged wisps and torn strips of darkness, very long thin arms ending in pointed shadowy fingers, a small round head with no features except two tiny glowing white eyes, a faint dark red mouth slit, semi-transparent edges, hovering slightly above the ground
```
**Negative prompt**
```
legs, feet, face details, colorful, solid opaque body, cute ghost, sheet ghost, cartoon
```
**메모**: 반투명 재질은 엔진에서 따로 설정하세요. 모델은 불투명하게 뽑고, 눈만 발광(emissive) 재질로 나누면 편해요.

---

## 5. 천장을 기는 것 (기계실)
몸길이 약 1.4m · 천장에 거꾸로 매달려 빛을 쫓는다

**Prompt**
```
A grotesque spider-like creature with a pale fleshy segmented body, about 1.4 meters long, eight long thin jointed humanlike legs ending in pointed tips, a ridged spine of bony bumps along its back, at the front a human face with a gaunt expression, sunken cheekbones, six small black eyes clustered on the forehead and face, and a wide black mouth, pale grey-pink skin with dark bruises, legs spread outward as if gripping a surface
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cute spider, fur, cartoon, insect shell, colorful, antennae, wings
```
**메모**: 게임에선 천장에 **거꾸로** 붙어 다녀요. 다리를 바깥으로 펼친 자세로 뽑은 뒤 엔진에서 뒤집으면 돼요.

---

## 6. 살덩어리 (기계실 · 마지막 방)
높이 약 1.8m · 느리지만 언제나 당신이 어디 있는지 안다

**Prompt**
```
A slow monstrous mass of glistening wet flesh, about 1.8 meters tall and 2 meters wide, dark red and purple pulsing tissue covered in veins, a dozen human eyeballs of different sizes embedded across its surface all looking in one direction, one large gaping mouth ringed with uneven yellowed human teeth, several smaller mouths, five pale human arms sticking out of the sides and dragging on the floor, slimy reflective surface
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cute slime, cartoon, legs, clean, dry surface, symmetrical, colorful
```
**메모**: 눈알은 따로 떼어진 오브젝트로 만들면 "모든 눈이 나를 따라 움직이는" 연출을 할 수 있어요.

## 7. 뭉치 (놀이방) — 마스코트 살인 로봇
키 약 2.65m · 가끔 전원이 꺼졌다가 다시 켜진다

**Prompt**
```
A terrifying abandoned daycare mascot robot, 2.65 meters tall, a rusty steel humanoid endoskeleton with exposed pistons, joints and dangling red, blue and black wires, draped in the rotten remains of a torn brown furry animal mascot costume that hangs in shreds from its chest, one arm still wearing a fur sleeve, one foot in a round fur costume boot and the other a bare steel foot with metal toes, its head is half costume and half machine: the right half is a torn furry bear-like mascot head with one round ear, a muzzle and a single dead glass eye, the left half is an exposed steel skull with two glowing red camera lens eyes, a mechanical jaw with rows of large sharp steel teeth, a dented chest plate with a small rusty brass name tag, blood and grime stains, standing in A-pose
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cute, clean, intact costume, cartoon, friendly smile, plastic toy, colorful new robot
```
**메모**:
- 이름표 글씨 「뭉치 · 놀이방 친구」는 AI가 한글을 잘 못 그려요. 이름표는 비워두고 나중에 텍스처로 넣으세요.
- 눈(카메라 렌즈)은 발광 재질로 분리해야 "전원이 꺼졌다 켜지는" 연출이 돼요.
- 아래턱은 따로 움직일 수 있게 분리해 주세요 (철컥철컥).

---

## 8. 기워진 것 (마지막 방 · 최종 보스)
키 약 2.8m · 버려진 장난감 공장의 잔해로 기워 만든 거인

**Prompt**
```
A towering emaciated nightmare creature assembled from toy factory scrap, 2.8 meters tall, hunched forward with a long neck pushing the head ahead of the body, a cracked and chipped porcelain skull-like head with deep black eye sockets holding tiny white pinpoint lights, a broken nose hole, a dangling hinged lower jaw with uneven yellow teeth, on the back of the head a second face with stitched-shut eyes and a vertical slit mouth lined with teeth, a bent rusty metal spine and broken ribs made of metal rods and pale doll plastic, three dead baby-doll faces fused into its chest and belly, several small doll arms poking out between the ribs as if grasping, scraps of rotten fabric and stuffing hanging off the frame, the left arm is a thin bony doll limb with five needle-like metal fingers, the right arm is an extremely long mechanical telescoping arm ending in a three-fingered hooked metal claw that drags on the floor, digitigrade backward-bending metal legs with claw toes, standing in A-pose
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cute, cartoon, clean, symmetrical arms, smiling face, plush toy, bright colors
```
**메모**:
- 머리는 앞뒤 두 얼굴이라, 머리만 따로 분리해서 **180도 돌아가게** 리깅하면 게임 연출 그대로예요.
- 오른팔 아래팔은 망원경처럼 늘어나게 **두 겹 파이프**로 나눠 주세요.
- 엔딩에서 셔터에 깔려 부서지니, **머리·팔·갈비뼈를 분리된 파츠**로 만들어 두면 부서지는 연출에 바로 쓸 수 있어요.

## 9. 아이 귀신 (놀이방, 해치지 않음)
키 약 1.3m · 막다른 곳에서 벽을 보고 울고 있다

**Prompt**
```
A small pale ghost child, about 1.3 meters tall, thin and frail, wearing an oversized dirty white hospital gown, long straight black hair hanging down covering the face, head slightly bowed, arms close to the chest with hands near the face as if crying, bare feet, faint grey-blue skin, standing still
```
**Negative prompt**
```
cute, cartoon, smiling, colorful clothes, shoes, visible face, adult
```
**메모**: 고개가 180도 뒤로 돌아가는 연출이 있으니 목 관절을 따로 두세요.
